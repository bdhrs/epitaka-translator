ePitaka Proofreading — read this first

One doc = one sutta. Each paragraph: a Pāli line (Kannada script), then its Kannada translation, then a blank line.

How to work
1. Sign in to your Google account, or you can only read.
2. Type your fixes straight into the translation. They appear as suggestions. If typing does nothing, set the pencil menu (top right) to "Suggesting".
3. Never change a Pāli line.
4. Keep the layout: Pāli line, translation line, blank line.
5. One suggestion per problem. Don't retype whole paragraphs.

Comments — in Kannada or English, whichever is easier.
- Wrong meaning: say what the Pāli means.
- Same term everywhere: "TERM: nibbāna → ನಿಬ್ಬಾಣ".
- A doubt: "QUESTION: …".
- Spelling and grammar fixes need no comment.

Finished a doc? Comment "DONE" on its first line.
