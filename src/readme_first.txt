ePitaka Proofreading — read this first

One doc = one book. A long book is split into parts: _1, _2 and so on. Each paragraph: a Pāli line (in the {language} script), a blank line, its {language} translation. Where an automatic review found a problem, the review remarks follow after a blank line. Two blank lines end the paragraph.

How to work
1. Sign in to your Google account, or you can only read.
2. Type your fixes straight into the translation. They appear as suggestions. If typing does nothing, set the pencil menu (top right) to "Suggesting".
3. Never change a Pāli line.
4. Keep the layout: Pāli line, blank line, translation, then two blank lines (review remarks, if any, sit between).
5. One suggestion per problem. Don't retype whole paragraphs.
6. The review remarks are hints from an automatic review. They can be wrong. Check them against the Pāli.

Comments — in {language} or English, whichever is easier.
- Wrong meaning: say what the Pāli means.
- Same term everywhere: "TERM: nibbāna → ನಿಬ್ಬಾಣ".
- A doubt: "QUESTION: …".
- Spelling and grammar fixes need no comment.

Finished a doc? Comment "DONE" on its first line.
